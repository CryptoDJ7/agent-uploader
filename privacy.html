Privacy Policy

Agent Uploader · effective November 21, 2026

Agent Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services; by using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

  • The identity of the connected YouTube channel (channel ID and title).
  • Metadata of videos the owner uploads through the tool (video ID, title, privacy status).

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's videos, set their thumbnails and change their privacy status after the owner's approval. No data is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth refresh token and a short upload log (video ID, title, date) are stored on the owner's private server with access limited to the owner. Nothing is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. No cookies or tracking technologies are used.

Retention and deletion

Upload logs are kept while the tool is in use and deleted on request or when the tool is retired. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions; revoking access makes the stored token useless, and the owner then deletes it.
