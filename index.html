Agent Uploader

A private publishing tool for one creator's own YouTube channels.

What it does

Agent Uploader is a small, self-hosted script used by a single independent creator to publish the animated educational videos he produces for his own channels (for example, Wild What Ifs). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

  • Uploads a finished video file with its title, description, tags and thumbnail (videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
  • Every upload starts as private. The creator reviews each video and personally approves it before it is made public (videos.update).
  • Reads only the creator's own channel identity (channels.list, mine=true) to confirm which channel is connected.

What it does not do

  • No access to other users' channels or data.
  • No analytics collection, scraping, comments, or advertising.
  • No sale or sharing of any data.

How access works

The creator signs in once with Google OAuth and grants access to his own channel. The resulting token is stored encrypted-at-rest on his own private server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
